## Chosen model

**`NYTK/sentiment-hts5-xlm-roberta-hungarian`**

Selection rule: best directional ECE is 0.493; 2/3 model(s) within 0.15 of it; among those the highest directional macro-F1 is 0.230 (NYTK/sentiment-hts5-xlm-roberta-hungarian).

`NYTK/sentiment-hts5-xlm-roberta-hungarian` has the strongest directional discrimination (directional macro-F1 0.230) among the candidates that are not pathologically over-confident (directional ECE 0.578, versus a best of 0.493). Because the pipeline aggregates soft probabilities rather than hard labels (ADR-0001/ADR-0003), a model that emits near-certain *wrong* directional scores is more damaging to the aggregate than one with slightly weaker per-post discrimination, so calibration gates the decision. Also calibrated enough but weaker on directional macro-F1: `cardiffnlp/twitter-xlm-roberta-base-sentiment` (0.170). Set aside for severe over-confidence (directional ECE more than 0.15 above the best): `NYTK/sentiment-ohb3-xlm-roberta-hungarian` (directional macro-F1 0.264, directional ECE 0.711).
