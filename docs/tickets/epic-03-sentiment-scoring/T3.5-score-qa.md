## Context

A degenerate model output (constant scores, obvious misclassification) would
poison the signal. Check before aggregating.

## Objective

Verify score distributions and spot-check quality.

## Implementation notes

- Histogram of `net` over time; check for suspicious flatness or bimodality.
- Spot-check ~50 posts (text vs score), especially from the `sarcasm`/`panic`
  eval examples.
- Compare the daily *mean* of `net` against intuition for a known crash day
  (2026-10-01) — it should be clearly negative.
