## Context

Raw per-post scores are noisy; the signal lives in their aggregation
(ADR-0003).

## Objective

Collapse posts into one row per Budapest trading day.

## Implementation notes

- Group posts by date in **Europe/Budapest** (convert `ts`).
- Per day compute:
  - `count` = number of posts,
  - `score_wmean` = Σ wᵢ·netᵢ / Σ wᵢ with **engagement weight**
    `wᵢ = 1 + upvotesᵢ` (weight scheme lives in config),
  - `bear_wmean` = engagement-weighted mean of `p_bear`,
  - `disagreement_std` = std of `netᵢ` (unweighted),
  - `entropy` = mean entropy of (p_bull, p_bear, p_neutral) across posts,
  - `z_score` = rolling z-score of `score_wmean` over a **60-day trailing
    window** (min_periods from config) — captures deviation from the forum's
    chronic baseline. The 60-day warm-up has no `z_score`; drop those rows
    (signals Parquet starts at day 61) so `z_score` stays non-null.
- Output the `signals` schema (T0.3).

## Acceptance criteria (DoD)

- [ ] Signals Parquet covers all trading days in the window.
- [ ] `z_score` uses only past data (no leakage into the future of its window).
- [ ] Sanity: 2026-10-01 (crash day) shows clearly negative `score_wmean` and a
      `z_score` below −1.
